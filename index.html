WHAT THE BENCHMARKS MEASURE

MMLU-g

general knowledge · 14,043 questions · 57 subjects

Multiple-choice across science, law, medicine, history, ethics. Generative variant: the model must produce the answer letter, extracted with a strict word-boundary regex. Tests knowledge recall and the ability to deliver it in the requested format.

GSM8K

multi-step reasoning · 1,319 problems

Grade-school word problems needing 2–8 arithmetic steps. A clean proxy for "can this model follow a logical procedure without derailing" — separates reasoning-tuned models from pure knowledge models.

IFEval

instruction following · 541 constraints

Instructions with programmatically checkable constraints (exact bullet counts, required words, length limits). Low IFEval + high MMLU = a knowledgeable model you cannot steer. The reliability metric for automation.

tok/s

throughput

Generation speed on a fixed smoke probe. On consumer VRAM, speed is a first-class citizen: a 4× faster model is often the right answer at a small quality cost.
